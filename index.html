OpenWrt for the Arcadyan AW1000

CleanWRT is an open build of OpenWrt 25.12 for the AW1000 5G router. It adds a working phone port (AWvoice), a modern interface (Apex) and LEDs that mean something. Everything is built in the open on GitHub.

Projects

CleanWRT

The firmware: OpenWrt 25.12 for the AW1000 with 5G, a phone port and the usual tools, all off until you ask.

Documentation · Source

AWvoice

Plug an ordinary desk phone into the RJ11 socket and make and take calls over your SIM (VoLTE), with caller id, hold and a call log.

Guide · Source

Apex

A dark and light LuCI theme with a sidebar, a phone-friendly drawer and card-style status widgets.

Details · Source

LED management

Drives the front-panel LEDs from live modem and network state, with a night mode. Works on its own on OpenWrt.
